How It Works

Whether you are hosting an event or casting a vote, FlexyVotes makes it seamless.

1. Find an Event

Browse active events on the homepage and click "View Event" to see the candidates.

2. Pay to Vote

Click "Vote for me", enter the amount you wish to contribute (₵1 = 1 Vote), and pay securely via PayStack.

3. Support & Win

Your contribution is instantly counted. Buy a ticket to the event to get free votes for your favorite candidate!

Create & Customize

Set up your event, add candidates, and fully customize the theme with your own Wine and Gold colors and background images. Create multiple categories to organize your contestants.

Choose Voting Mode

Select "Pay-to-Vote" for fundraising (where ₵1 = 1 Vote), or "Code Voting" for free, fair elections. Generate secure codes manually or upload a CSV of Student IDs to prevent duplicate voting.

Sell Tickets & Manage

Sell event tickets online with premium branded E-Tickets and QR codes. Use the built-in Mobile Scanner App to check in attendees at the door and prevent ticket fraud.

Track & Earn

Watch live analytics, export CSV guest lists, and generate PDF reports. Enable "Tie-Breaker" voting to incentivize online ticket sales, and sell award plaques directly through your store!
